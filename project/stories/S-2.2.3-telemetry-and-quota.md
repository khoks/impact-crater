## Description

Per ADR-0015 — append-only JSONL telemetry at `~/.impact-crater/telemetry.jsonl` with `LLMCallEvent` records emitted on every provider call (cache hits emit cost=0). Per-job aggregation into `JobCostSummary` persisted at `snapshots/{snapshot_id}/cost_summary.json`. Pre-job dual-cap quota check (total + per-provider) against the `quota_state` SQLite table. Rate cards as YAML files under `config/rate-cards/`.
