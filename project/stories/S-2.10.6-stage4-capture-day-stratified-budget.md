## Description

As a user, I want the densest, most-photographed parts of my trip represented proportionally — not starved because one location's shots happened to out-rank them globally. In the SW-US-trip job, Bryce (the single largest location cluster, 333 shots) got only 4 candidates' worth of attention while Grand Canyon (238) dominated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The final Stage 4 selection replaces the global `ranked[:target_size]` with a stratified proportional allocator keyed on capture-DAY: proportional quota + largest-remainder rounding + a small per-bucket floor (`min_per_bucket=1`), ranked within each bucket by combined_score.
- [ ] Must-keep destination-coverage shots (S-2.10.5) are inviolable SEEDS inside the allocator (one shared budget mechanism — no double-budgeting).
- [ ] Deterministic (stable bucket iteration + tie-break); `sum(selected) == target_size`; reservations clamped to the ADR-0011 floor/ceiling.
- [ ] Offline A/B on snapshot b4b73c7b1fe044b7: Bryce coverage rises, Grand Canyon still gets the most clips (presence, not parity), overall quality not visibly worse.

## Notes

Higher regression risk than the rest: a proportional budget can buy balance at the cost of quality by pulling clips from weak days and capping strong ones. Mitigation: `min_per_bucket=1` (guarantee PRESENCE, not parity), generous proportional share, and an offline A/B against the known-good 23-shot arc before merging. This is why it is split out from S-2.10.2/S-2.10.5 into its own atomic PR. See ADR-0017.
