## Description

Populate and apply the three PlanDirective levers in initial planning: positional
duration control (brief-derived, e.g. "slower opener"), tempo-aware photo
duration/frequency from the song's energy curve/sections, and the optional
standard-mode soft-align of clip boundaries to section starts (subsumes the
S-2.11.6 optional criterion). Density shaping ships off (duration-only first cut).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `build_directive_from_music` derives a `TempoProfile` (song halves; higher-energy half → shorter photos) and populates `soft_align.section_starts_ms`.
- [ ] Positional rules apply per timeline region with neighbour shoulder; total stays within target after scale.
- [ ] Soft-align nudges boundaries within window; no-op in music-video mode.
- [ ] `apply_density` defaults False (no clip-count change) until a gated follow-on.
