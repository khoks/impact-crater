## Description

As the owner reading a job's cost summary, I want "saved by cache" to reflect what the cached calls would really have cost, so that the figure can be trusted next to the real spend. `telemetry.summarize_job` sets `estimated_cost_saved_by_cache_usd` to (sum of this job's miss costs ÷ miss count) × hit count. On a warm re-run the only misses are the Opus judge and a couple of cents-level calls, so every Haiku / embedding cache hit is priced like a third of a judge call: the S-2.10.9 Zion re-run reported **$31.97 saved** for 392 hits whose cold cost was ≈ $0.13 (snapshot `dffc5bc48484467b`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each cache hit is priced per operation — from the rate card and the cached entry's recorded usage, or from the per-op ballpark (`router._ballpark_cost`) — not from the job-wide average miss
- [ ] A unit test with a warm-run event mix (one judge miss, many Tier-S / embedding hits) asserts a savings figure within the cold run's real cost
- [ ] Wherever the UI shows the figure, it reads the corrected value
