## Description

As a user who lists destinations in my brief ("...through Zion, Bryce, Horseshoe Bend, Grand Canyon, and Las Vegas"), I want the video to actually touch every place I named — or be told honestly when a place was barely photographed — instead of a named destination being silently dropped. This is the headline fix for the SW-US-trip job, where ALL 37 Nevada-area shots (incl. a good Hoover Dam landmark) were dropped before the judge ever saw them. Resolves S-2.9.20.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A real brief-parse step runs in the analysis path (Stage 0.5, after ingest, concurrent with Stage 2), producing `BriefIntent{theme, must_include, named_destinations[name/aliases/kind/chronological_hint], chronological, ...}` via the existing `parse_user_brief` route + a `BRIEF_SCHEMA`. Fail-soft (empty intent → current behaviour). Cached by brief hash.
- [ ] `map_destinations()` matches each named destination to assets: text (name/alias vs caption + location_description), optional GPS reverse-geocode of photo clusters, and a same-capture-day bridge to attach driving videos. Reports `basis` (matched/sparse/none) honestly.
- [ ] Stage 4 deterministically RESERVES the top 1–2 assets per named destination (by max(specialness, combined_score)) before the cut, tags them `dest=<name>`, and emits a single `destination_coverage`/CoveragePlan object on `CandidateSet`. Reservation is bounded (≤2×#destinations) and clamped to the envelope ceiling.
- [ ] The judge prompt's coverage block (already added, gated on `named_destinations`) renders from the CoveragePlan and requires ≥1 beat per destination-with-media, else an honest open_question.
- [ ] Re-run the SW-US-trip job: the Hoover Dam shot (or another Vegas-leg shot) reaches the judge and a Vegas beat appears (or arc_reasoning honestly notes Vegas was barely photographed).

## Notes

The must-keep reservation (bounded, additive) is the part that fixes Vegas and is lower-risk than the full stratified budget (S-2.10.6) — they must share ONE budget/merge mechanism (must-keeps as inviolable seeds inside the stratifier) to avoid double-budgeting. Offline reverse-geocoder (`reverse_geocoder`, no network) is optional — text+day-bridge covers this trip without it. The judge-prompt coverage block already ships (S-2.10.3) and no-ops until this lands. See ADR-0017.
