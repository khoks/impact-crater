## Description

As the user, I want to see every decision each pipeline phase made (what
media was kept/dropped and why, how the arc was ordered, the final clips,
the trip cast) and attach correct/incorrect/should-differ feedback to any
of them, stored where Claude can pick it up and act on it later.

## Notes
