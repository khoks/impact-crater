## Description

As a user, I want to optionally add a splash/title card at the start of my video that captures its spirit — the main people composited onto an AI-generated background image, with a fitting title and the year — chosen at job creation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A job-creation toggle "Add a title card" (+ optional title text; year auto-derived from capture dates).
- [ ] When enabled, a Stage-6.x step generates an AI background image via a **remote image-gen API** (per ADR-0007's remote abstraction; D-054), composites the trip's main people (from the A-018 cast: top group members' best crops) and a title + year, and prepends it as the first clip (~2.5–3.5s, gentle Ken Burns).
- [ ] Fail-soft: if image-gen fails/quota-denied, fall back to a typographic title card over a representative photo — never block the render.
- [ ] Cost surfaced in the pre-job estimate; gated by quota.

## Notes

Decision (D-054): the background is generated via a **remote image-gen API**, not a local model — simplest, no local weights, small per-image cost within quota, consistent with ADR-0007. A text-to-image route is added to the router (new operation, e.g. `generate_title_background`). Compositing (faces + text over the AI image) is deterministic (PIL/ffmpeg). The "spirit" prompt is derived from the brief theme + locations + season. Feedback #7.
