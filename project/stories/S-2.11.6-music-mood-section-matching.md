## Description

As a user, I want the AI to understand my music's mood, tempo, and section structure and match the photos/clips to its sections — calm scenery under calm passages, energetic/people/motion under the drops — even in standard mode, not just beat-snapped music-video mode.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Music analysis is extended with per-section mood/energy descriptors (valence/energy/tempo class), beyond the current bpm + energy_curve + sections.
- [ ] The Stage 5 judge receives the section structure + mood in standard mode (today the section/energy detail is only surfaced in music_video mode) and is instructed to align clip mood (calm landscapes vs energetic/motion/people) to the song's arc.
- [ ] Optionally, Stage 6 aligns segment boundaries to section starts in standard mode (soft, not hard beat-snap).

## Notes

Design: `media/music.py` already computes bpm, bpm_stability, sections, energy_curve via librosa. Add lightweight mood/energy descriptors per section (energy + spectral features → calm/building/peak). Thread the analysis into the standard-mode judge prompt (currently gated on `mode == "music_video"`) and add a "match mood to the song's sections" instruction. Heavier mood models (CNN taggers) are a later option. Feedback #8.
