## Description

As the owner, I want my decision-level verdicts (correct / incorrect / should-be-different on Stage 4 keep/drop, the Stage 5 selection, Stage 6 clips and the cast, stored in the `feedback` table with `phase`, `decision_ref`, `content_hash`, `verdict`, `comment`) exported into a golden set, so the eval harness reports agreement with my judgement and not only with internal metrics. Today the feedback table is read only by `scripts/feedback.py` for triage; nothing turns it into a test oracle. Per A-029 and the restart plan Phase 2 item 2 ("owner-labelled golden set exported from the feedback table").

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `scripts/eval_plan.py export-golden` (or `scripts/feedback.py export`) writes `tests/fixtures/eval/golden/<trip>.json` with `{phase, decision_ref, content_hash, verdict, comment, snapshot_id}` per item, excluding `dismissed` items and never including screenshots
- [ ] The harness reports agreement per phase — for each golden item, whether the evaluated plan/diagnostics makes the same decision (kept / dropped / selected / in cast) — with a summary precision; the `pytest -m eval` check is informative at first and becomes a gate once ≥ 2 trips are labelled
- [ ] ≥ 2 trips are labelled (SW-US and Zion); the owner's labelling session is recorded in the activity log; the rows triaged by S-2.13.3 are the seed
- [ ] Golden items are keyed by content hash and decision, not by snapshot, so re-labelling after a re-run keeps history
