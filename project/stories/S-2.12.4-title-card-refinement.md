## Description

Two coupled pieces: (1) the flagged follow-up — refinement re-plans from the arc
and drops a post-plan title card, so it must be **re-injected** on refine; and (2)
let the user **refine the title card itself** in natural language — its **text**,
its **background image** (style/spirit), the **placement** of the title, and its
**appearance** (colour/size, show year/faces). Both need a persisted
`TitleCardSpec` — the follow-up preserves it, the user edits it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `TitleCardSpec` (title_text, style/spirit, title_position, text_color,
  text_size_scale, show_year, show_faces) persisted on `RenderPlan`; the raw AI
  background persisted (`title_card_bg.png`) so text/placement edits don't require
  a new image-gen call.
- [ ] `build_title_clip` honours the full spec (placement, colour, size, faces,
  image style) and can reuse a provided background.
- [ ] Refinement re-injects the title card as clip 0 on every refine when the
  parent had one (fixes the drop-on-refine follow-up).
- [ ] The refinement planner interprets title-card requests: change the text
  ("call it 'Desert Wandering'"), change the image ("make it a painterly sunset"),
  move the title ("put the title at the top"), restyle it ("bigger white title,
  no year") — merging a `title_card_patch` and re-rendering a child snapshot.
- [ ] Text/placement edits reuse the existing background (no new image cost);
  image edits regenerate. Fail-soft throughout.
