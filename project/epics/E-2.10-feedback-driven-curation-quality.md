## Goal

Close the quality gaps found by inspecting the real 1,663-asset Southwest-US-trip job (snapshot `b4b73c7b1fe044b7`) against its media and submitting per-decision feedback. The job produced a polished 108s arc (conf 0.85) but: dropped **Las Vegas entirely** (a named destination), selected **zero video** in a "highlight video", stacked **six near-identical family-at-sunset shots** at the climax, **under-covered Bryce** (the most-photographed place) while over-covering Grand Canyon, never used the **specialness** signal it pays Tier-M to compute, **over-classified the trip cast** (81 "group"), opened **backwards in time**, and shipped a **feedback UI that hangs forever on submit**. This Epic makes the curator brief-aware (named-destination coverage), balanced (per-location + people/landscape + variety), video-inclusive, specialness-aware, and gives reviewers a working, complete inspect/feedback loop.
