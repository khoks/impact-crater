## Goal

Convert the `ArcJudgment` from E-2.2 into a rendered Story Video MP4. Stage 6 plan compile (deterministic — without orchestrator second-guess yet, that lands in M6) + Stage 7 ffmpeg-driven render. Standard music mode (background music under the video) — no music-video sync yet (that lands in M4).

This is **M2**. After E-2.3 closes, feeding test set + brief + audio + target_duration produces a real MP4.

## Scope

- Stage 6 plan compiler: walk `selected_items`; compute clip durations; insert simple cuts (no music-video beat-snap yet); validate against target_duration; persist as `snapshots/{snapshot_id}/plan.json`.
- Standard music mode: audio mixed under entire video at -16 LUFS; fade in over 1.5s at start; fade out per the agentic music-duration handler (defaulting to fade-out at MVP since orchestrator agentic-handler lands here too).
- Music-duration analysis stub (Madmom/librosa lands in M4; M2 just gets duration from ffprobe).
- Stage 7 render: ffmpeg subprocess execution per the plan; H.264 / yuv420p / AAC; YouTube-friendly defaults (1080p / 30fps for 16:9).
- Aspect-ratio handling: 16:9 only at MVP; pad-or-letterbox 9:16 sources.
- Smart-crop integration via `smartcrop.py` for photos.
- Two-pass loudnorm at the final encode step.
- Worker-pool concurrency: max 1 concurrent render per job.
- API endpoint `POST /api/jobs/{id}/render` that takes a `snapshot_id` (from Stage 5) + audio path + visibility metadata and returns the path to the rendered MP4.
- Snapshot `cost_summary.json` aggregated and persisted per [ADR-0015](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0015-resource-accounting.md).

## Out of scope

- Music-video sub-mode (M4)
- Beat detection + section detection (M4)
- Section-to-media NL mapping (M4)
- Orchestrator second-guess pass (M6)
- UI surfaces (M3)
- Publishing the rendered MP4 (M7)
