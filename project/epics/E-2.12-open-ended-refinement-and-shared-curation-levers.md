## Goal

Make the **refinement phase** able to take in *arbitrary, unpredictable*
natural-language requests and act on them — not just re-run the judge with a
brief addendum. The user must be able to say things like: "insert more photos of
Las Vegas"; "don't drop Las Vegas even if its quality/impact is low"; "make the
clips in the beginning 50% longer / 2s longer and reduce the photos around them
by 0.5s"; "increase photo duration/frequency with the song's rising tempo in the
later half, decrease with the falling tempo" — and more we can't predict. The
prompt engineering must let the agent *think* from the request + the analysis
already computed, and perform *more* analysis on the media only when needed, then
regenerate the video accordingly.

Delivered by building two **shared curation levers** used by both initial
planning and refinement, then an **agentic refinement loop** whose tools are thin
wrappers over them:

1. **PlanDirective** (ADR-0019) — the single duration/positional/tempo shaping
   contract Stage 6 consumes and refinement emits (positional emphasis,
   tempo-aware pacing, soft section-align).
2. **Destination reservation** (`ReservationSet`, S-2.10.5) — a source-agnostic
   "must-keep" mechanism through Stage 4 (the Vegas fix), reused by refinement's
   `reserve_destination`/`force_include` and by A-023 feedback.
3. **Agentic refinement** (S-2.12.3) — a bounded tool-use loop that maps a free
   request onto {reserve, adjust_durations, edit_narrative, reanalyze_media,
   regenerate} and re-renders a child snapshot.
