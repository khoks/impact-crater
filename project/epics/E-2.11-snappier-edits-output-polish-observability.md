## Goal

A second round of user feedback on the SW-US-trip re-renders: the edits are too slow and too thin. Make the curator produce **snappy, fully-covering** edits (2–3s clips, every sub-location/time represented, no single viewpoint hogging the cut, no jerky <2s video flashes, no 5–8s held photos), add **output-polish** features the user asked for (an opt-in AI title/splash card; an occasional rapid burst-montage for dense same-backdrop clusters; music-mood/section-aware matching), and give the user **much deeper observability + feedback** (job- and phase-level feedback, and a richer live view of the modules/decisions executing — at the depth of the NotebookLM pipeline infographic).

## Scope

- S-2.11.1 — Snappier, fuller edits: per-clip duration caps (photos 1–3s, never 5–8s), min-video eligibility (≥2s), coverage of every distinct location+time, per-viewpoint cap. [feedback #1,2,3,4,6] — **implemented this session**
- S-2.11.2 — Feedback at the **job** and **phase** level, not only per-media decision. [#9]
- S-2.11.3 — Richer live **module/decision** progress visualization (the infographic's depth). [#10]
- S-2.11.4 — **Burst-montage** clip type: 0.5s photos squished into a 2–4s sequence for dense same-backdrop clusters (≤30 min, same place). [#5]
- S-2.11.5 — Opt-in **AI title/splash card** (remote image-gen): main people composited on an AI background with title + year, prepended. [#7]
- S-2.11.6 — **Music mood/tempo/section** analysis → match media to song sections (beyond music-video mode). [#8]

## Out of scope

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A 120s re-render uses ~40–60 clips; no photo > 3s; no video < 2s; every distinct GPS location/time present; no viewpoint > ~3 clips.
- [ ] The user can leave feedback on the whole job and on a phase, not just a single media decision.
- [ ] The live job view shows the individual modules/decisions executing, not just "Step N of 7".
- [ ] (Opt-in) a title/splash card can be prepended; (occasional) a burst-montage can represent a dense cluster; music sections inform matching.
