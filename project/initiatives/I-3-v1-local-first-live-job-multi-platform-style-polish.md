## North-star outcome

The app is the differentiated product: it runs local-first on capable hardware, ingests media live during an event, publishes to Instagram/Facebook/X as well as YouTube, learns the user's style from reference media, and ships the power-user polish bundle.

## Why now

MVP proved the single thin slice; v1 turns it into something materially better than generic AI media tools, leading with the highest-novelty differentiators (local-first, live job, style learning) per D-039.

## Scope

Local-first LLM; live job; multi-platform connectors; reference-style learning; power-user polish; auto editing; music sourcing expansion; LLM-driven profile; full cache.
