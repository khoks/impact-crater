## North-star outcome

The app runs on mobile, a multi-agent harness powers conversational refinement and the autonomous Trip Package (dump a whole trip → get a coherent set of videos/reels/montage), and music can be generated.

## Why now

Once single-video quality is mastered (D-042 gate) and v1 differentiators ship, v2 delivers the dump-and-delight north star and the mobile + conversational experience. The 2026-10-24 restart plan opens this initiative only on the gate-met D-NNN (D-058 measured by E-3.10) and starts with E-4.5 as a deterministic pilot; mobile, multi-agent, chat, generated music and crowd removal are explicitly parked behind the package shipping.

## Scope

Full mobile UI; multi-agent harness; conversational refinement at scale; generated music; the Trip Package autonomous multi-artifact planner; AI crowd removal.

## Out of scope

Hosted multi-tenant mode (I-5); anything that lands before the Phase 6 gate.
